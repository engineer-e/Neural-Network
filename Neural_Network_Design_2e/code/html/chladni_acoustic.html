Physics of Pattern Formation & 3D Cymatics

1. 3D Surface Flexure & Wave Motion:
In 3D mode, the metal plate geometry physically deforms dynamically according to standing acoustic wave height:

$$z_{\text{plate}}(x, y, t) = A \cdot J_n\left(k_{n,m} r\right) \cdot \cos(n \theta) \cdot \sin(2\pi f_{\text{drive}} t)$$

2. 3D Grain Bouncing Physics & Gravity:
Sand grains on antinodes receive vertical kinetic energy boosts proportional to amplitude. When airborne ($z > z_{\text{plate}}$), grains follow parabolic trajectories under gravity:

$$\frac{d^2 z}{dt^2} = -g, \quad \vec{v}_{xy} \rightarrow -\nabla w(x,y) \cdot P_{\text{resonance}}$$
  • 3D Nodal Accumulation: Airborne particles land back on the surface, sliding down displacement gradients until settling on stationary nodal lines ($w=0$).
  • Resonance Line Width: Quality factor $Q$ controls how tightly particles concentrate into sharp geometric patterns.
